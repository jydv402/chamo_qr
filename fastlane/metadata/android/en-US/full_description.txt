Chamo QR is a powerful and elegant QR code utility designed for speed and privacy.

Features:
- Lightning-fast QR and Barcode scanning.
- Create custom QR codes for URLs, text, Wi-Fi, and more.
- Save and manage your scan history locally.
- Beautiful, intuitive interface with dark mode support.
- Fully offline functionality—your data never leaves your device.
- Open source and privacy-focused.

Scan with confidence and generate with ease with Chamo QR.
